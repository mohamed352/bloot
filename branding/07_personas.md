# Bloot — User Personas

## Persona 1: The Casual Player

### Ahmed — "اللي يلعب مع ربعه"

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Name** | Ahmed Al-Harbi |
| **Age** | 30 |
| **Location** | Riyadh, Saudi Arabia |
| **Occupation** | Marketing manager |
| **Device** | iPhone 15 Pro |
| **Balout Experience** | Played since childhood, weekly games with friends |
| **Tech Comfort** | High — uses social media, watches streams |

### Ahmed's Story

Ahmed has played Balout since he was 12 — his father taught him at family gatherings. Now, Ahmed plays weekly with his college friends at a diwaniya. Sometimes they can't all meet in person (someone's traveling, someone's busy), so they want a way to play remotely that still feels like being together.

Ahmed doesn't care about rankings or tournaments. He wants a simple, reliable way to play with his 3 friends, hear their voices, and talk smack from across the city.

### Goals

1. **Create a room in 30 seconds** and share the link with friends
2. **Hear his friends clearly** — voice is non-negotiable
3. **See their faces (optional)** — camera is nice but not required
4. **Play a proper Balout game** — correct rules, correct scoring
5. **Get back to real life** — finish a game, close the app

### Frustrations

- Voice chat on other apps is laggy and drops
- Existing Balout apps feel like they were designed in 2012
- Setting up a game takes too many steps
- Friends who don't play often give up because the interface is confusing

### Bloot Features Ahmed Needs

| Feature | Priority | Reason |
|---------|----------|--------|
| Quick room creation | P0 | Must be fast and easy |
| Voice chat | P0 | The whole point of playing together |
| Video chat | P1 | Nice to have, makes it feel real |
| Correct Balout rules | P0 | No compromise on game accuracy |
| Share room link | P1 | Easy way to invite friends |
| Simple score display | P0 | Clear, not confusing |

### Design Implications

- **"العب" button must be front and center** — one tap to start
- **Voice should connect automatically** — no setup required
- **Room sharing should use WhatsApp/iMessage** — meet Ahmed where he is
- **Score display should be visual and simple** — no complex stats, just "who's winning"

## Persona 2: The Competitive Player

### Faisal — "اللي يبي يفوز دايم"

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Name** | Faisal Al-Dosari |
| **Age** | 25 |
| **Location** | Kuwait City, Kuwait |
| **Occupation** | Software engineer |
| **Device** | Samsung Galaxy S24 Ultra |
| **Balout Experience** | Daily player, competitive, analyzes strategy |
| **Tech Comfort** | Very high — early adopter, stats nerd |

### Faisal's Story

Faisal is serious about Balout. He plays daily — online and in person. He tracks his stats, studies strategies, and wants to be the best. He cares about his ranking, his win rate, and his tournament performance. He wants Bloot to be the platform where real Balout competition happens.

Faisal hates playing with people who don't take the game seriously. He wants matched opponents, fair play, and visible proof of skill.

### Goals

1. **See my ranking** — where do I stand?
2. **Compete in tournaments** — organized, scheduled, with prizes
3. **Track my stats** — win rate, average score, best partners
4. **Know the game is fair** — anti-cheat, transparent rules
5. **Find skilled opponents** — matchmaking based on skill level

### Frustrations

- People who quit mid-game
- Suspected cheating in existing apps
- No proper ranking systems
- No organized tournaments
- Can't see detailed stats about my play history

### Bloot Features Faisal Needs

| Feature | Priority | Reason |
|---------|----------|--------|
| Ranking system | P0 | Must prove skill |
| Tournament system | P1 | Competitive outlet |
| Detailed stats | P0 | Must track performance |
| Anti-cheat indicators | P0 | Must know game is fair |
| Skill-based matchmaking | P1 | Better games, less frustration |
| Player reputation system | P2 | Avoid quitters and bad actors |

### Design Implications

- **Profile page must show ranking prominently** — Faisal checks this daily
- **Stats should be detailed and visual** — charts, graphs, trends
- **Tournament banners on home screen** — always visible, always exciting
- **Anti-cheat badge visible on room lobby** — reassures competitive players
- **Post-game stats** — show more than who won; show strategy breakdown

## Persona 3: The Streamer/Host

### Noura — "اللي تبث وتجمع الناس"

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Name** | Noura Al-Kaabi |
| **Age** | 28 |
| **Location** | Dubai, UAE |
| **Occupation** | Content creator / social media influencer |
| **Device** | iPhone 15 Pro Max |
| **Balout Experience** | Skilled player, charismatic host, growing audience |
| **Tech Comfort** | Very high — streams regularly, knows the tools |

### Noura's Story

Noura doesn't just play Balout — she performs it. She streams her games to her growing audience, comments on strategy, cracks jokes, and makes her viewers feel like they're sitting at the table with her. She's the life of the digital diwaniya.

Noura needs tools to entertain her audience while staying focused on the game. She needs viewer interaction, gifting, follower growth, and analytics. She treats Bloot as both a gaming platform and a broadcasting platform.

### Goals

1. **Stream my game to an audience** — professional-looking, reliable
2. **Interact with viewers** — chat, react, acknowledge gifts
3. **Grow my following** — make it easy for viewers to follow me
4. **Earn from streaming** — gifts, subscriptions, sponsorships
5. **Look good on camera** — lighting filters, camera controls

### Frustrations

- Existing gaming platforms don't support card game streaming well
- Can't see chat while playing the game
- No built-in audience tools (no follow system, no gifting)
- Generic streaming platforms (Twitch) don't cater to Arabic/Balout audiences
- Switching between game app and streaming app is terrible UX

### Bloot Features Noura Needs

| Feature | Priority | Reason |
|---------|----------|--------|
| One-tap streaming | P0 | Must be easy to go live |
| Viewer chat | P0 | Must see and respond to viewers |
| Gifting system | P1 | Revenue and engagement |
| Follower system | P0 | Must grow audience |
| Stream analytics | P1 | Viewers, watch time, peaks |
| Camera controls | P1 | Look good on stream |
| Stream schedule | P2 | Let viewers know when to tune in |

### Design Implications

- **"ابث مباشر" (Go Live) button must be prominent** — Noura's most important action
- **Viewer chat overlay** — visible during game, doesn't block play
- **LIVE badge system** — viewers need to see she's live
- **Follower count on profile** — social proof
- **Gift animations** — need to be visible but not disruptive to game
- **Streamer tools accessible** — stats, camera, chat toggle — all one tap away

## Persona 4: The Viewer

### Sara — "اللي تتفرج وتتعلم"

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Name** | Sara Al-Mansoori |
| **Age** | 22 |
| **Location** | Doha, Qatar |
| **Occupation** | University student |
| **Device** | iPhone 14 |
| **Balout Experience** | Beginner — watches to learn, doesn't play yet |
| **Tech Comfort** | High — watches streams daily, social media native |

### Sara's Story

Sara is new to Balout. She's watched her family play at gatherings but doesn't fully understand the rules. She follows Noura's streams because they're entertaining and educational. Sara watches, asks questions in chat, and dreams of playing someday — but right now she's shy about joining a game and making mistakes.

Sara is Bloot's future player. Today she watches. Tomorrow she plays.

### Goals

1. **Find entertaining streams** — browse by category, streamer, or popularity
2. **Learn the game** — understand rules, strategies, common plays
3. **Chat with the community** — feel welcomed, not judged
4. **Follow favorite streamers** — get notified when they go live
5. **Eventually start playing** — when she feels ready

### Frustrations

- No good Balout streams with Arabic commentary
- Existing apps have no viewing mode — you have to play
- Can't easily learn rules in existing apps
- Feels intimidated to join a game as a beginner

### Bloot Features Sara Needs

| Feature | Priority | Reason |
|---------|----------|--------|
| Stream discovery | P0 | Must find streams easily |
| Viewer mode (no play) | P0 | Must be able to just watch |
| Chat during streams | P0 | Must interact with community |
| Follow streamers | P0 | Must know when favorites are live |
| Rules/learn section | P1 | Must understand how to play |
| Beginner-friendly rooms | P2 | Must eventually feel safe to play |

### Design Implications

- **Home feed must show streams prominently** — Sara's primary entry point
- **"تفرج" (Watch) tab is as important as "العب" (Play)** — viewers are first-class users
- **Chat must be welcoming** — emoji reactions, positive culture
- **"تعلم" (Learn) section** — rules, strategies, beginner guides
- **Low-pressure entry** — "spectate" option on rooms, no commitment to play
