Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

1. Introduction

Bloot ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us, including:

  • Account information: your name, email address (from Sign in with Apple or Google), username, and profile picture.
  • User content: messages you send in chats, and avatars or photos you upload.
  • Game statistics: your match results, level, and leaderboard progress.
  • Usage & device data: product interactions, crash logs, and performance diagnostics (via Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics) to improve our services.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to provide, maintain, and improve our services, to communicate with you, to match you with other players, and to ensure fair gameplay.

4. Voice and Video Data

Voice and video calls during gameplay are processed in real-time (via Agora) and are not stored on our servers. We do not record or retain your conversations.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share aggregated, anonymized data for analytics and improvement purposes. We do not use your data for cross-app advertising tracking.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information. You can:

  • Edit your profile at any time in the app (Profile → Edit).
  • Permanently delete your account and all associated data in the app (Settings → Danger Zone → Delete Account). Deletion is processed immediately, including server-side data.
  • Contact our support team for any data request.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@bloot.app.
