Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a private room?

From the Home screen, tap Create Room, choose your settings, and share the invite code with your friends. They can join from Home → Join Room by entering the code.

How do I enable voice or video in a room?

Inside a room, tap the microphone or camera icon. Your device will ask for permission the first time — please allow it. Your mic is muted and camera off by default when you join.

How do I report a user or offensive content?

Tap on the user's profile (from a room, stream, or chat) and choose Report. Our moderation team reviews every report.

How do I block someone?

Open the user's profile and choose Block. Blocked users' messages, streams, and content will be hidden from you. You can manage your blocked list in Settings → Privacy.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings → Danger Zone → Delete Account. This permanently removes your account and all associated data, including server-side data. This action cannot be undone.

The Discover tab is empty — is something wrong?

Discover shows live streams happening right now. If no one is streaming at the moment, it will be empty — check back later or start your own stream!
